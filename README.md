Entry for the bounty "the word you'd delete from the dictionary".

**Entry word: `queue`**

## Why this word fits each judge criterion

1. **C1 – Real word.** "Queue" is a common English word everyone recognises.
2. **C2 – Instant.** Five letters, four of them silent. The joke lands the moment you see the spelling.
3. **C3 – Humour from the word itself.** Its spelling is literally a queue of letters standing behind one loud Q. It sounds exactly like "cue". Deleting it erases the very concept of lining up.
4. **C4 – Fresh.** It stands out against the flooded field. "moist", "literally" and "irregardless" will dominate the entries.
5. **C5 – Exact.** Exactly one word, spelled correctly.
